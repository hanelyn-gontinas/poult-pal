📖 How to Take Care of Your Chicken

Learn the basics before starting your responsibility!

🌾

Feeding

Provide enough food at the appropriate times. Make sure your chicken does not stay hungry.

💧

Drinking Water

Always provide clean drinking water. Refill the water when it runs out.

💊

Vitamins

Follow the indicated care schedule for the vitamins in the game.

🌙

Sleep and Rest

Give your chicken enough time to rest. Use the lights to manage its sleeping environment.

💚

Stress and Comfort

Maintain a comfortable environment. Avoid conditions that may increase your chicken's stress.

🎮 Game Rules

  • Take care of your chicken for 3 days.
  • Each in-game day lasts 24 real-world minutes.
  • Monitor your chicken's needs regularly.
  • Earn points by providing proper care.
  • Neglecting your chicken may lead to Game Over.
  • Your final score depends on your chicken's condition.
